Set up Claude

  1. Download the Claude desktop app and sign in.
  2. Click the Code tab at the top. Not the normal chat. The normal chat can't touch your computer.
  3. Make a new, empty folder called local-ai (on your Desktop is fine) and pick it as the project folder.
  4. Leave permissions on the default, Ask permissions. Do not pick bypass or auto mode. We want it asking before every command. That's the whole point.

Set up ChatGPT

  1. Download the Codex app and sign in with your ChatGPT account.
  2. Make a new, empty folder called local-ai (on your Desktop is fine) and open it in Codex.
  3. Leave approvals on the default. Do not switch to full access. We want it asking before every command. That's the whole point.
✓

02

Step two

What's under the hood?

Before we download anything, we need to know what your computer can handle without crying. This step is look, don't touch. The AI only reads info about your machine. It doesn't install or change a thing.

The one rule

The whole AI setup (the model, the engine running it, and its memory of your chat) has to fit in 80% of your RAM. The chat memory (called "context") gets 50% of the model's size. Leaves breathing room so your computer doesn't turn into a space heater.

Or just do the math right here

How much RAM have you got? (Mac: Apple menu → About This Mac. Windows: Settings → System → About.)
